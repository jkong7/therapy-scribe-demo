1 Consent & capture

Privacy caveat: live transcription uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Some browsers (e.g. Chrome) send audio to the browser vendor's servers for processing under their own terms. This app keeps no audio and sends nothing anywhere itself, but it cannot control what the browser/provider does. If unsure, paste a transcript instead.
